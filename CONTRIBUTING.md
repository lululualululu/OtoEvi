# Contributing

Use an issue to report reproducibility problems or discuss a change. Include the commit, configuration, seed, environment and a minimal synthetic reproducer. Do not upload patient images, reports, credentials or local checkpoints.

Run the unit tests and result-consistency check before submitting a pull request. Changes to the model, loss, preprocessing or metric definitions need a test that distinguishes the intended behavior from the previous one. Keep reported paper results tied to their frozen provenance; do not overwrite them with a modified recipe.

By contributing, you agree to release your contribution under this repository's MIT license and confirm you have the rights to do so.
